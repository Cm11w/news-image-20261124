# Research notes — world news, Tuesday 24 November 2026

How this page was put together: I read the morning wires and market
closes on Tuesday, picked the forty stories that mattered most across
regions and beats, and drew them as one newspaper page. The lead is the
Nvidia board bringing Jensen Huang back. Everything below is what the
page shows, in my own summary.

## The lead

- Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as chief
  executive, eleven days after he stepped down. The shares jumped 31%
  in a day — the best session since 2003 — adding about $952 billion
  of market value. The Senate Banking Committee set a hearing for
  December 3, and the SEC is looking at the company's accounting.

## Markets

- Wall Street had its strongest rally since April 2025: Nasdaq up
  6.5%, S&P 500 up 4.7%, Dow up 3.0%.
- Anthropic shares slipped while other AI names rallied; the stock
  trades near $86 after listing at $83 on November 19.
- India's Sensex fell 1,484 points to 82,435 and the Nifty lost 451.
- Indian factory activity picked up speed in November as the private
  sector strengthened.
- Bitcoin ran to an intraday high of $64,505 — up 17% from $55,130 —
  before settling near $61,327 at Monday's close.

## United States

- Chicago O'Hare stopped departures briefly after a staffing shortage
  at the regional control centre, in the middle of the Thanksgiving
  travel rush.
- Donald Trump attacked California governor Gavin Newsom in a Justice
  Department speech, over Newsom commuting 548 death sentences.
- A federal appeals court opened a path for Trump to have his
  hush-money conviction erased.
- The former mayor of Jackson, Mississippi, was sentenced to 37 months
  in prison in a bribery case tied to a proposed hotel.
- Federal agents raided child-care centres in Minnesota as part of a
  $41 million fraud investigation.
- Allegheny County certified its midterm election results, confirming
  steady turnout.

## Europe and Ukraine

- NATO's North Atlantic Council held an extraordinary session on
  November 23; Poland called in the Russian ambassador.
- NATO chief Mark Rutte visited Warsaw while allies weighed their next
  moves on Ukraine.
- Denmark's highest court backed the hardline housing measure known as
  the "ghetto law".
- Around 450 schools in Germany received bomb threats sent by email.
- Russia is lining up $576 million of rail ferries to keep Kaliningrad
  supplied around European restrictions.
- President Zelenskyy said Ukraine is ready for four-way peace talks.
- Russian drone and missile strikes killed several people in the Kyiv
  region, hitting homes in the village of Dymer.
- Reports say the Kremlin is preparing to put nuclear weapons in orbit.
- Ukrainian drones sank a Russian corvette off Libya in a strike
  launched 2,400 km away; a second Russian ship caught fire near
  Finland after a drone attack.

## Middle East

- An Israeli strike in Gaza killed a UN aid worker and wounded three
  colleagues.
- Bombing in Gaza City killed at least three civilians, Palestinian
  media reported.
- The United Nations condemned air strikes on Khartoum that are
  thought to have been launched from Ethiopian soil.

## Asia

- A fire at a candy factory in Indonesia killed at least 24 people;
  rescuers are still searching for around 30 missing workers.
- China announced targets for 2030 to strengthen care for the elderly
  and support for childcare.
- A Chinese official was sentenced to death for leaking 100
  intelligence reports, 35 of them state secrets.
- Prime Minister Narendra Modi met Nepal's prime minister Balendra
  Shah to talk about energy, trade and transport links.
- Indian and Chinese commanders held border talks in Arunachal Pradesh.
- Modi opened an artificial-intelligence centre at IIT Patna, with a
  research park worth Rs 400 crore.
- Lahore topped the world's pollution ranking again, with air quality
  readings above 500.
- South Korea said suspected North Korean aircraft entered its airspace.
- Bangladesh vowed to arrest former prime minister Sheikh Hasina if
  she returns from India.
- Bangladesh's leader Muhammad Yunus blamed people at a higher level
  as Grameen Bank branches were attacked.

## Climate, science and sport

- The COP31 climate talks ended without agreement on stronger wording
  for phasing out fossil fuels.
- A supermoon is due to meet the Pleiades — the Seven Sisters — in
  tonight's sky.
- Carlos Alcaraz won his first ATP Finals title, beating Alexander
  Zverev in Turin.
- India's D. Gukesh began defending his world chess title against
  Javokhir Sindarov in Geneva.
- The first Test between India and New Zealand ended in a draw at
  Wellington's Basin Reserve.
- FIFA's congress chose the hosts for the 2031 and 2035 Women's World
  Cups.
